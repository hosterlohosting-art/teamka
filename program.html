Martial arts programs · Marietta

Three disciplines. One practical system.

Build real self-defense, fitness, confidence and composure through Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu, Tae Kwon Do and Muay Thai.

Technique for the body. Resilience for life.

Every class develops two things at once: a body that can respond and a mind that can remain composed. Students learn practical techniques in a structured, respectful environment.

Programs are planned for adults and children ages eight and older. Beginners are welcome, and experienced martial artists can continue sharpening their skills.

Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu

Control, escapes, leverage and composure for staying safe when a confrontation reaches the ground.

Tae Kwon Do

Precision, balance, movement and powerful technique taught with patience and discipline.

Muay Thai

Effective striking, conditioning, distance and confidence adapted for responsible real-world self-defense.
