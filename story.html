Our story · Marietta, Georgia

Courage began on the mat.

Ibrahim Awad's martial-arts journey started at thirteen. The discipline learned there still shapes how he leads, serves and stands up for others today.

More than 25 years of lessons, passed forward.

Ibrahim earned his black belt at sixteen and has fought in more than 500 matches. Training taught him to prepare completely, remain composed under pressure, protect people and keep moving forward.

Those lessons followed him into the courtroom and inspired Team Karate Attorney: a welcoming Marietta school where children and adults can build practical skill, earned confidence and lasting resilience.

The goal is bigger than technique. Every class is designed to help students become stronger, calmer and more courageous in everyday life.
