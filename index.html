01 — The story

Discipline in the dojo. Courage in the courtroom. Purpose in everything.

Ibrahim Awad’s life is built at the intersection of service and strength. From beginning karate at thirteen to becoming a respected trial lawyer, TEDx speaker and community leader, every chapter carries one lesson: protect people, pursue purpose, and never lower your standards.

Today, he leads a growing team while teaching the principles that shaped him — preparation, patience, belief and the discipline to keep your hands up.
